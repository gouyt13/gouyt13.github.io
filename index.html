About

I am a PhD student in the College of Computing and Data Science at Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Cheng Long. I received my bachelor’s degree from Tsinghua University.

I study vector databases, with a focus on vector quantization and approximate nearest-neighbor search.

Featured open-source project

RaBitQ Library

Lead developer & primary maintainer

Official RaBitQ library with an asymptotically optimal space–error trade-off, SIMD acceleration, and integrations in txtai and zvec.
